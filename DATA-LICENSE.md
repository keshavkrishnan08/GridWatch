# Data licensing

The **code** in this repository is MIT-licensed (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

The **civic data** compiled in [`/public/data`](public/data) — facility records,
docket references, and derived figures — is released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Use it, cite it, build on it; just credit GridWatch Indiana.

Source documents (IURC filings, county records, utility filings, news reporting)
remain the property of their respective publishers and are cited per record. See
[METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md) for how each figure is sourced.

Some auto-discovered layers carry their upstream terms: OpenStreetMap data is
ODbL, and PeeringDB records are CC BY 4.0. Attribute those upstreams if you
republish them.
